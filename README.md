# Slayers At UA

Demo: https://khanaaaaaa.itch.io/slayers-in-ua
Click F for fullscreen.

## What It Is
A fanmade visual novel that crosses Demon Slayer (Kimetsu no Yaiba) with My Hero Academia. You play as someone who knows both worlds as you read the mangas, and wake up inside it. First, Demon Slayer and then My Hero Academia. The story follows Muichiro Tokito and you as you two navigate UA High School after being pulled from the Demon Slayer world.

## Why I Made It
I was very inspired by the game Class of '09 while making this. Researched and figured out Ren'Py exists, and this is what came out of it. Made this purely for fun, with a plot line I read in a fanfiction when I was younger.

## How I Made It
**Tech Stack:**
- Ren'Py

The project uses Ren'Py's label/jump system for putting together different parts sice I was struggling with keeping track in one script file, with a route variable that tracks player personality to adjust dialogue tone throughout.

## What I Learnt And What I Struggled With
First of all, this is my first prject in Ren'Py so I learnt a lot about it. The biggest thing I felt like I learnt is the route variable that affects the dialogue line another character will say. The biggest struggle was maintaining sprite consistency across scenes. I changed my mind with what version to choose across all the dialogues, but decided to used a aged up character after some part so it was a hassle to redo the sprite names in the script. I also struggled with pacing, took some advice from friends while making this, and decided to make it a bit more rapid. Overall, the process was very fun and loved learning about all the tips and tricks in Renpy.
